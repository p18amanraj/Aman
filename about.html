A bit about me

I started my career on a steel plant floor in Jamshedpur and have spent most of it running large programs where the deadline doesn't move.

My story

How I got here

My first job was at Tata Steel in Jamshedpur, on the team that brought a new coke plant to full capacity in 35 days. That is where I learned to care about cycle times, root causes and fixed schedules.

After an MBA at IIM Indore, I ran medical services operations at Pristyn Care, then spent nearly four years at Dhruv Research running election surveys in Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Delhi and Bihar.

In 2025 I took a break to prepare for the UPSC Civil Services. I cleared Prelims 2026 and wrote the Mains. Alongside, I invest full time and trade part time in Indian equities, and I post my weekly picks on the Markets page.
