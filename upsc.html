Fifteen months studying how India is governed

In May 2025 I stepped away from work to prepare full time for the Civil Services Examination. I cleared Prelims 2026 and have written the Mains. I'm open to roles while the result is pending.

What it added

How it shows up in my work

The year was not a pause from my career. It made me better at the parts of it that matter most.

  • Policy and governance depthI read electoral data and government programs with the context of how the state actually works.
  • Writing under time pressureMains answer writing trains you to make a clear, structured argument in a few hundred words. That is most of executive reporting.
  • Discipline over a long horizonA fixed exam calendar, a daily plan and weekly self-review for over a year. The same habits I used on election timelines.
